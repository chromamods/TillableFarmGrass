# Releases

## 1.5 (2026-11-06)
- Flower grass can be tilled, cleared and blended like the rest of the grass.
- With Generic Mod Config Menu, a Restore button turns all the grass you cleared back into grass.

## 1.4 (2026-11-06)
- Hit farm grass with a pickaxe to clear it to dirt. Hit it again to bring the grass back.
- Soft dirt edges on every shade of grass, in every season and with recolour mods.
- No more straight grass edges when tilling diagonally or next to paths and stepping stones.

## 1.3 (2026-11-05)
- Tilled grass blends into the grass around it with soft edges instead of a hard square.
- One more half-grass edge tile can be tilled.

## 1.2 (2026-11-05)
- Tilled grass goes back to grass when the soil is gone, whether you clear it with a pickaxe or it dries up overnight.

## 1.1 (2026-11-05)
- Half-grass edge tiles can be tilled too.
- Tilled grass turns into plain dirt and stays dirt.

## 1.0 (2026-11-04)
- First release.
- Permanent grass on every farm map can be tilled.
